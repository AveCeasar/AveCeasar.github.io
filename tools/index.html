Members tools

Backtests on the full ChartVault daily archive. They run in your browser on data that unlocks with the members link in any paid NASDUCK issue. Not a member yet? Get NASDUCK paid.

  • DCA calculator What would regular buys of SPY or QQQ be worth now? Weekly to quarterly buys since 1993 (SPY), 1999 (QQQ) or 1928 (S&P 500 index), with an optional buy-and-hold comparison.
  • Lump sum vs DCA Put it all in now, or spread it out? Both approaches tested from every start month, with how often each won and by how much.
  • Drawdown recovery How long until I'm back to even? Every big fall, how deep it went and how long the recovery took, plus a break-even check for any buy date.
  • Retirement withdrawals Would the money have lasted? A monthly income raised each year, tested from your start date and from every other start month.
  • Portfolio rebalancing What does a mix of stocks, bonds and gold do? SPY, QQQ, IWM, TLT and gold at any weights, rebalanced on a schedule, against one benchmark.
  • Sell after a drop Does selling after a fall and buying back later pay? Three buy-back rules, every round trip, time out of the market and the result against staying invested.
