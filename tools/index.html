Members tools

Backtests on the full ChartVault daily archive. They run in your browser on data that unlocks with the members link in any paid NASDUCK issue. Not a member yet? Get NASDUCK paid.

  • DCA calculator What would regular buys be worth now? Weekly to quarterly buys of SPY, QQQ, the S&P 500 index since 1928, gold, bitcoin or any of 500+ stocks, with yearly raises, fund fees and a buy-and-hold comparison.
  • Buy the dip Wait for a dip, or buy every month? Monthly savings invested at once, against held in cash (earning T-bill interest) until the price falls 5% to 30% below its high.
  • Lump sum vs DCA Put it all in now, or spread it out? Both approaches tested from every start month, with how often each won and by how much, and optional T-bill interest on waiting cash.
  • Drawdown recovery How long until I'm back to even? Every big fall, how deep it went and how long the recovery took, plus a break-even check for any buy date.
  • Returns heatmap What did every start and end year return? A grid of returns for every buy year and sell year, plus the worst, typical and best 1 to 30-year holds.
  • Seasonality Do some months really do better? Average, median and hit rate by calendar month and weekday, and November-April against May-October.
  • P/E and later returns What did a P/E like today's lead to? Every month since 1928 grouped by the S&P 500's P/E, with the returns over the next 1 to 20 years.
  • Retirement withdrawals Would the money have lasted? A fixed, balance-based or guardrails income from one asset or a two-asset mix, tested from your start date and every other start month.
  • Portfolio rebalancing What does a mix of stocks, bonds, gold and cash do? US, international and small-cap stocks, Treasuries, gold, silver, bitcoin, T-bill cash and one stock, rebalanced on a schedule or on drift, with monthly deposits.
  • Sell after a drop Does selling after a fall and buying back later pay? Three buy-back rules, trading costs and T-bill interest while out, every round trip and the result against staying invested.
