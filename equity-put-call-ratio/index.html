How To Read It

  • Higher readings usually mean traders are leaning harder toward puts in single-name options, which often lines up with defensive sentiment or stress.
  • The 10-day smoothing matters because the unsmoothed daily ratio is noisy enough to throw off the bigger signal.
  • This is most useful at extremes and inflections, especially when it starts confirming or diverging from credit spreads and volatility charts.
