How To Read It

  • The 3-month yield is closest to current policy, while the 2-year reflects where markets expect policy to move next.
  • The 10- and 30-year yields add longer-run growth, inflation, and term-premium expectations to the picture.
  • A normal curve has longer yields above shorter yields; an inverted curve has one or more short maturities above long maturities.
