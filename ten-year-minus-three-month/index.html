How To Read It

  • Negative readings mean the 3-month yield is above the 10-year yield and this section of the curve is inverted.
  • A move back toward zero or above it is re-steepening, but the full curve board shows whether short yields fell, long yields rose, or both.
  • The direction and speed of the move matter more than treating any single threshold as a timing signal.
