How To Read It

  • When the S&P 500 line keeps separating higher, global leadership is still concentrated in U.S. equities.
  • If EFA or EEM starts outperforming while U.S. breadth weakens, that usually signals a real regime shift rather than a one-day headline.
  • Use this with the dollar and rates pages because global relative performance often turns when currency and liquidity pressure changes.
