How To Read It

  • Big positive readings mean a sector is stretched above trend; deep negative readings mean it is washed out below trend.
  • Use it to spot potential mean-reversion setups as well as trends that may be running too hot.
  • The most useful signals often come when extreme readings start reversing, not just when they print.
