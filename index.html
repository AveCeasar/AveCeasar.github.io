Market Tape Snapshot

Four quick inputs explain the tape below: breadth, leadership, risk pricing, and the yield curve. The longer interpretation stays in the letters.

Breadth Broad S&P 500 participation

66.2% are above the 20DMA and 72.2% are above the 200DMA, so the average stock is still confirming the move.

 Leadership Defense + Energy 30D sector leadership

Technology, Energy, and Healthcare are leading, which reads like selective exposure with an inflation or growth hedge.

 Risk Pricing Calmer VIX versus realized vol

VIX is 14.3 versus 13.0 realized volatility, leaving a 1.3-point fear premium in options.

 Yield Curve Positive 10Y minus 2Y

+0.51% keeps the curve clearly above zero, which is a cleaner macro backdrop than inversion.

Participation is still doing real work underneath the index. 66.2% of S&P 500 members are above the 20DMA, 69.1% are above the 50DMA, and 72.2% are above the 200DMA, which is the profile of a rally with broad enough confirmation.

Technology (+9.0%), Energy (+7.8%), and Healthcare (+7.3%) are leading the 30-day sector board. Energy plus defensives is usually a sign that investors still want exposure, but with a hedge against softer growth or stickier inflation.

The fear premium has cooled materially: VIX sits at 14.3 versus 13.0 realized volatility. At the same time the 10Y minus 2Y curve is +0.51%, which is materially cleaner than inversion. Net: the macro backdrop is less hostile, but the internal tape still deserves respect.
